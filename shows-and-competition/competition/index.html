Competition

Competition is another way to grow as a dancer: practicing with purpose, working together, and sharing a performance on stage. From rehearsals to the final bow, the experience is about learning, expression, and being part of a team.

Sample information

The Team

A competition team brings dancers together to strengthen their technique, develop stage presence and support one another. Teamwork, steady progress and a love of performing are at the heart of the experience.

Rehearsals

Rehearsals give dancers time to learn choreography, refine details and become comfortable performing as a group. Consistent practice, preparation and communication help every dancer contribute to the team.

Events

Competition weekends offer a chance to perform, receive feedback and celebrate progress together. Dates, venues, arrival times and performance schedules help families prepare for each event.

Getting Ready

Pack your costume, dance shoes, water and any hair or makeup supplies you need. Review the event schedule in advance, arrive ready to warm up and support your teammates throughout the day.

Costs

Competition participation may involve entry fees, costumes and travel. Review the season’s cost breakdown and payment schedule before committing, and ask about any additional expenses.

Questions

Interested in the competition experience? Contact the studio to discuss your dancer’s goals and learn about team placement, preparation and the next steps.
