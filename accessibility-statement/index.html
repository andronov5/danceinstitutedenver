Accessibility Statement

Last updated: November 5, 2026.

Dance Institute is committed to making information about our classes, events, and registration accessible to people with disabilities. If something on this website prevents you from getting the information or help you need, please contact our team.

Our accessibility goal

Our goal is to work toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. These guidelines help make websites easier to perceive, navigate, understand, and use with assistive technology.

The website has not completed a comprehensive accessibility evaluation, so we do not claim full WCAG conformance. This statement describes the features currently implemented and the limitations we know about.

Features that support access

  • Headings, navigation landmarks, and a Skip to Main Content link help visitors move through each page.
  • Navigation controls, gallery buttons, and form fields have text labels and keyboard focus indicators.
  • Informative images have text alternatives; decorative images are marked accordingly.
  • The weekly schedule is an HTML table with row and column headings.
  • Browser zoom is available, and the design adapts to different screen sizes.
  • Scroll effects and automatic gallery movement respect your device's reduced-motion preference. Galleries and testimonials also stop automatic movement while their controls have keyboard focus or the pointer is over them.

Known limitations and alternatives

  • Small-screen schedule: the full week is shown together, which makes text small on a phone. Browser zoom or a larger screen may help. Call or email us for help finding a class or for the schedule in another format.
  • Moving galleries: galleries and testimonials do not currently have a persistent pause button. Enable Reduce Motion in your device's accessibility settings to stop automatic advancement, or keep keyboard focus within the gallery while reading.
  • Third-party registration: Sawyer's embedded registration and checkout are provided separately, and we do not control every part of their accessibility. Contact the studio if you have trouble completing a task.
  • Contact form: submitting prepares an email draft and requires an email application. If it does not open, email the studio directly or call us.

New photos and edited content may introduce additional barriers. Please tell us about missing image descriptions, hard-to-read content, or controls that do not work for you.

Using the website

The site uses standard HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Use a current browser and any assistive technology you normally use. JavaScript is needed for live content updates, interactive galleries, and the embedded registration experience; the site's basic information and contact links remain available without those features.

Our review has included source-code and functional checks. Compatibility has not been comprehensively verified across browser and screen-reader combinations, and not every editable color or layout state has been tested.

Request help or report a barrier

Email dance@danceinstitutedenver.com or call or text 303-525-0011. Please include the page link, what you were trying to do, the difficulty you encountered, and how you would prefer us to contact you. You may include your browser or assistive technology if that would help; you do not need to share a diagnosis.

Our team can help explain class schedules and program information and discuss an alternative way to complete your task. For a time-sensitive registration or show question, please call. If your concern remains unresolved, contact us again and ask for studio management to review it.

For questions about visiting the studio or accessibility at a performance venue, please contact us before your visit so we can discuss the location and your needs. This website statement does not certify the accessibility of a building or venue.
