ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT

At Dance Institute, we are dedicated to ensuring our dance studio is welcoming and accessible to everyone, including individuals with disabilities. We strive to provide an inclusive environment that empowers all students to express themselves through dance.

This statement was last updated on [enter relevant date]. We at Dance Institute are committed to making our studio and website accessible to all individuals, ensuring everyone can engage with our dance programs.

What web accessibility is

An accessible platform allows everyone, regardless of ability, to participate in our dance classes and activities with ease and enjoyment. Our goal is to accommodate all students, ensuring they have equal access to our resources.

Accessibility adjustments on this site

We have adapted our website in accordance with WCAG 2.1 guidelines and have made it accessible to the level of AA. Our site is compatible with assistive technologies, such as screen readers, to enhance the user experience for all visitors.

  • Utilized accessibility tools to identify and rectify issues
  • Set the language for our site
  • Organized content for easy navigation
  • Established clear headings on all pages
  • Provided alternative text for images
  • Ensured color contrast meets accessibility standards
  • Minimized motion effects on the site
  • Made sure all multimedia content is accessible

Accessibility arrangements in the organization

Our studio locations are equipped with ramps and designated parking for individuals with disabilities. We ensure that all spaces, including classrooms and restrooms, are accessible and welcoming. Additional support services are available upon request.
