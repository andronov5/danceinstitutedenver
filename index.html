01 About Dance Institute

About
Dance Institute

At Dance Institute, we are passionate about teaching dance to students aged 2 to adult. Our extensive curriculum includes Ballet, Tap, Lyrical, Contemporary, Pointe, Hip-Hop, Poms, Acro, Tumbling, and Jazz. We create a nurturing environment where students not only learn to dance but also develop confidence, dedication and self awareness.
