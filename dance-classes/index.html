Dance Classes

Combo Classes

3-4 Year Old

A one-hour combination of ballet, tap and tumbling for ages 3–4.

4-5 Year Old

A one-hour combination of ballet, tap and tumbling for ages 4–5.

5-7 Year Old

A one-hour combination of ballet, tap and tumbling for ages 5–7.

6-9 Year Old

A 75-minute combination of ballet, tap and jazz for ages 6–9.

8-11 Year Old

A 90-minute combination of ballet, tap and jazz for ages 8–11.

Advanced Classes

Jazz 1

A one-hour jazz class for ages 9 and up focused on rhythm, coordination and technique.

Jazz 2

One hour of level 2 jazz technique and combinations for ages 11 and up.

Jazz 3

One hour of level 3 jazz technique and combinations for ages 12 and up.

Jazz 4

One hour of level 4 jazz technique and combinations for ages 12 and up.

Contemporary/Lyrical 1

A one-hour contemporary and lyrical class for ages 9 and up combining expressive movement and technique.

Contemporary/Lyrical 2

One hour of level 2 contemporary and lyrical dance for ages 11 and up.

Contemporary/Lyrical 3

One hour of level 3 contemporary and lyrical dance for ages 12 and up.

Contemporary/Lyrical 4

One hour of level 4 contemporary and lyrical dance for ages 12 and up.

Ballet Classes

Ballet/Pre-Pointe

A 90-minute class for ages 9 and up combining ballet technique with preparation for pointe.

Intro to Pointe/Pointe 1

A one-hour introduction to pointe technique for dancers ages 12 and up.

Pointe 2

One hour of level 2 pointe technique for dancers ages 12 and up.

Pointe 3

One hour of level 3 pointe technique for dancers ages 12 and up.

Advanced Pointe Technique

A 90-minute technique class for dancers in Pointe levels 2 and 3.

Hip-Hop Classes

Hip-Hop Intro

A 45-minute introduction to hip-hop rhythm and movement for ages 5–8.

Hip-Hop 1

One hour of hip-hop technique and choreography for ages 8–11.

Hip-Hop 2

A 75-minute class in level 2 hip-hop technique and choreography for ages 11 and up.

Hip-Hop 3

A 75-minute class in level 3 hip-hop technique and choreography for ages 11 and up.

Additional Classes

Trot'n Tots

A 45-minute creative movement class exploring rhythm and movement.

Musical Theater

A one-hour class combining Broadway-style jazz and tap.

Leaps, Turns, Tricks Adv 1/2

Technique practice in leaps, turns and dance tricks for advanced levels 1 and 2.

Leaps, Turns, Tricks Adv 3/4

Technique practice in leaps, turns and dance tricks for advanced levels 3 and 4.

Intro to Poms

A one-hour introduction to pom dance, arm motions and coordinated movement for ages 9 and up.

Adult Tap

A tap class for adults focused on rhythm, footwork and coordination.

Class placement is determined by Dance Institute Instructors.
