Our Story

✦

At Dance Institute, we are passionate about nurturing dancers of all ages, from toddlers to adults. We offer a diverse range of classes including Ballet, Tap, Lyrical, Contemporary, Pointe, Hip-Hop, Poms, Acro, Tumbling, and Jazz. Our annual performance of the Nutcracker Ballet and vibrant Spring Recital showcase our talented students. We strive to cultivate a well-rounded dancer in a supportive environment, prioritizing health and safety while fostering friendships and essential life skills such as confidence, resilience, and dedication.
