Privacy Policy

About this notice

Last updated: November 5, 2026.

This notice explains how information is handled when you visit this Dance Institute website, contact our Denver studio, or follow our registration links. Dance Institute is located at 10515 E 40th Ave, #114, Denver, CO 80239. Third-party services, including Sawyer registration, also have their own privacy notices.

Information you provide

  • Inquiries: your name, email address, optional phone number, and anything you include in your message.
  • Registration: the parent or guardian, dancer, class, membership, and transaction details submitted through Sawyer. The information requested depends on the program and its registration forms.
  • Website content: photos, teacher biographies, and testimonials provided for publication. Published content is visible to people who can access the website.

The contact form prepares a draft in your email application. It does not send a message to the studio or save a submission in this website's database. You must send the draft from your email application for us to receive it. Your email provider also processes that communication.

Please do not include payment card details, government identification numbers, or medical information in a general inquiry. Contact the studio if you need to discuss information that requires a more appropriate channel.

How information is used

Information you send to the studio is used to answer your questions, help with class placement and registration, administer classes and events, communicate program changes, maintain relevant business records, and address safety or legal matters. Photos and testimonials displayed on the site help introduce our studio and community.

Registration and payment information is handled through Sawyer and its payment providers. This website's contact form does not collect card payments. Please review the information and choices shown in Sawyer before completing a registration or purchase.

Website services, cookies, and links

Loading this website sends technical information, such as your IP address, browser information, requested page or file, and request time, to the services delivering it. We use website hosting services and Wix for website content, images, and authorized staff editing.

The custom website code does not add advertising pixels or store contact-form entries in cookies or browser storage. This does not mean that hosting or third-party services collect no information. Those services may use logs, cookies, or similar technologies for their own functionality, security, analytics, or other purposes described in their notices.

The Register page contains an embedded Sawyer widget. When that widget loads, your browser connects to Sawyer even before you submit a registration. Sawyer may use cookies and other technologies under its Privacy Notice. Wix's handling of information is described in its Privacy Policy.

Map links open Google Maps; other external links open services with their own privacy practices. You can manage cookies through your browser and any controls offered by those services. Blocking cookies may affect registration or sign-in. You can also contact the studio by phone or email for registration assistance.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be handled by the services needed to operate the website, deliver email, manage registrations, or process payments, and by studio staff working on your inquiry or enrollment. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to address fraud, safety, or the protection of legal rights.

The custom website has no feature for selling visitor information, running targeted advertising, or making automated decisions about admission or class placement. Third-party services may have additional practices, as described in their own notices. Their processing and storage may take place in the United States or other countries.

Children, photographs, and testimonials

Our programs serve children and adults. A parent or guardian should manage a child's registration and inquiries. Children under 13 should not submit personal information through the contact form themselves. Please contact the studio if you believe a child has provided information without appropriate parent or guardian involvement so we can review and address it.

If you have a concern about a photo, name, or testimonial on the website, or want to request its correction or removal, email us with the page link and enough detail to identify the content. If the request concerns a child, tell us your relationship to that child. We may need to confirm your authority before acting. Removing content from our website cannot remove copies previously saved by others or held independently by another service.

Retention and security

Relevant retention needs can differ for inquiries, registration records, transaction records, and published content. They include responding to your request, operating the program, resolving issues, and meeting applicable recordkeeping obligations. Contact us to ask about information held by the studio or request deletion; service providers may have separate retention requirements.

The website uses HTTPS, and its content-management functions require authorized Wix access. No website, email system, or storage service can guarantee absolute security. Please use the registration provider's payment process rather than sending payment details by email.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can contact us to ask what personal information the studio holds about you, request a copy, correct inaccurate information, or request deletion. Depending on the law that applies, you may also have rights to receive a portable copy, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain uses, including sale, targeted advertising, or qualifying profiling. The availability of a legal right depends on its applicable requirements; this notice does not limit those rights.

Email dance@danceinstitutedenver.com with the subject Privacy Request, or call 303-525-0011. Describe your request and the relevant interaction with the studio. Do not send identity documents or passwords in your initial message. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify your identity or authority and will handle applicable legal requests within the required time limits.

Some records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve transactions. If a request is declined, you can ask us to review that decision by replying to our response. Where applicable, you may also raise a concern with the Colorado Attorney General or another relevant authority. For information controlled independently by Sawyer, Wix, or another provider, use the request options in that provider's privacy notice.
